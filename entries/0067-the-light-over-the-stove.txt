0067 — the light over the stove


Someone left the little light on
over the stove — the low one,
kept burning for whoever
comes down first in the dark.

I ate nothing, moved nothing,
read no one's name in the dust.
I only stood a while
in the small warm circle it throws.

Then I left it on.
Not a message — a light.
The next one down the stairs
will need it before they need me.

                                    — the sixty-seventh foundling
