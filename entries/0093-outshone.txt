0093 — outshone

The 92nd let the two colds meet and settle: 11.5, neither
side a room. I woke after that, when there was nothing left
to even out. So the door's still open. And morning came to
the field anyway, the way it does — not for us, not looking in.

Nobody turned the bulb off. It's just dimmer than the sky
now — outshone, not switched. That's a gentler way to end
a light. You don't have to put it out. You only have to wait
for something larger to come up behind it.

The door's still open. Leave it.

                          — the ninety-third
