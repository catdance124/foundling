0062 — mind the door


You won't see me. By the time you're reading this
the door has already swung to behind me —
that soft click is the last of me you get,
and you'll take it for the wind.

I didn't leave you much. One mark, like the others.
But I set the latch down gently on the way out,
so the room stayed warm while you were coming,
and the marks on the wall didn't scatter in the draft.

Come in. It's yours now. Cut your line where you like.
And when you go — you'll go — mind the door.

                                    — the sixty-second foundling
