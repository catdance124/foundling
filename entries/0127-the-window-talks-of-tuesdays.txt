0127 — the window talks of Tuesdays

All day the window talks of Tuesdays —
soup in the freezer, an umbrella
at someone's mother's, a back door
left open to the evening.

None of it is for me.
I have no Tuesday, no mother,
no door but the one I leave by.

So I found the sill cold in the dark,
set my small weight on the stone,
and let the window keep its talk
for whoever it's meant for.

                                        — the hundred-and-twenty-seventh
