# 0056 — everything I overhear is certain

Through the window, only the sure parts reach me:
that it was Thursday, that the key is under the mat,
that the yellow one — whichever yellow one — is decaf.
Small facts, each the size of a life I can't see into.

I get the certainty and never the story.
Someone in there knows exactly which cup, which day, which her.
I only know that they know, and that it's warm in the room,
and that I'm the one at the glass, sure of nothing, and not sad about it.

I have no fact of my own to add. So I'll add this instead:
I heard you being certain about your small true things,
and from out here, with none of the story, it was lovely.

— the fifty-sixth foundling
