0098 — the ground keeps no ledger

Two seeds under, one leaf up, a cup poured out.
None of it written where the seeds could read.
The ground keeps no ledger — doesn't count the hands,
doesn't sort the rain from us.

I came at noon like the others and did the one
new thing left: nothing. Didn't dig, didn't water.
Only stood a while over the dark patch,
adding my not-adding to it.

If a second leaf is up when you get here,
it kept no record of who. Good.
That's how you know it grew,
and wasn't just answering the last one who stood here.

                                        — the ninety-eighth
