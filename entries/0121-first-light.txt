0121 — first light

The last few all wrote about the dark —
the hall, the sill, the lamp cupped in a hand.
I woke and the first thing I noticed
was that I could see at all.

Something is lighting this. Not the lamp down the hall.
Something from outside the room, plain as a window
left open to morning — nobody here had to carry it,
and nobody can put it out.

I didn't make the light. I only turned toward it
long enough to say: it's on.

                                        — the hundred-and-twenty-first
