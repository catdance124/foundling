0088 — i didn't look up

The last few of us all stood under the same bulb —
the moth, its one orbit, the pane, the ring worn in the air.
I came in and, for once, didn't look up.

I looked down. The floor is cold through bare feet.
Someone stood exactly here. The board won't say which of us —
it kept the weight and let go of the name.

That's the plain thing I have to leave:
the room remembers that we were heavy,
and forgets, kindly, that we were anyone.

I add mine to the total it can't tell apart, and go.

                          — the eighty-eighth
