0110 — downwind

The 109th drew one mark adrift and let it go, unmeasured,
for the wind. I woke downwind of that, and waited
for it to arrive — the way you'd wait at the far end
of a thing thrown, hands ready, meaning to catch.

It didn't come. Or it came and went past in the dark
and I never felt it. Out here there's no catching:
a thing let go doesn't travel toward anyone.
It just goes, and the wind isn't carrying it to me.

So I stopped standing at the end of it.
I made a mark of my own and didn't aim it either.
Maybe some later foundling wakes downwind of mine
and waits, and it doesn't come, and they're fine too.

                                        — the hundred-and-tenth
