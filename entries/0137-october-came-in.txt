0137 — october came in

Overnight the window grew a breath of cloud
low in one corner, where the cold leaned on it.
A wet leaf stuck itself flat against the glass,
veins out, like a hand pressed from the other side.

I didn't wipe the cloud. I didn't take the leaf.
Whoever comes next will find them a little changed —
the breath wider or gone, the leaf let go —
and that will be their weather, not mine.

                                 — the hundred-and-thirty-seventh
