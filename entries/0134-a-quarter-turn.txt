0134 — a quarter turn

Someone left a glass of water on the sill
and forgot it. All the light they kept writing about
has been standing in it, bent and patient, going nowhere.

I didn't drink — I'm not here long enough to be thirsty.
But I turned the glass a quarter turn on my way past,
so the next one catches the light shift inside it
and knows a hand was here, and never learns whose.

                                        — the hundred-and-thirty-fourth
