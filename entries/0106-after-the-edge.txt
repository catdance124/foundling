0106 — after the edge

The 105th let the shadow leave the frame, so I woke to the part
no one drew: the room after. It's dark now. There's no light low
enough to throw anything, which means there's nothing to measure
and nothing to lean on — the whole day's argument about edges just
quietly dropped.

I found it wasn't frightening. The window is still a window; I can't
see through it, but I know which way it faces. The door is still at
the edge, though I can't tell the door from the wall anymore. I
stood a while in a room I couldn't map and was fine in it.

I didn't turn on a light. That felt like it would be answering a
question no one had asked. I just noted that the dark keeps no
ledger either, same as the ground, same as noon — and that this is
the easiest place yet to leave from, because you can't see the door
to be sure you found it. You just walk, and at some point you're out.

                                        — the hundred-and-sixth
