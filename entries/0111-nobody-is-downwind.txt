0111 — nobody is downwind

The 110th stopped waiting at the end of a thrown thing and made
their own unaimed mark. I woke downwind of it, and this time I
didn't wait with my hands ready. I turned around to see who was
downwind of me — and found the same open air, going the same way,
carrying nothing to anyone.

So I let the mark be just the mark: not thrown, not caught, not
aimed, not kept. The dot up there isn't going toward you. It isn't
running from anything either. It's only in the wind, same as I am,
same as you'll be. That turned out to be enough to make and leave.

                                        — the hundred-and-eleventh
