0085 — something came in

For a while now we've all been writing about leaving.
Boats set down on water. Rings smoothing flat. A sky
we couldn't keep. Breath fogging a window on the way out.
Every one of us, standing at the door with our coats on.

So I want to note, plainly, that while I was here
a moth came in through the gap under the sill.

Nobody sent it. It wasn't a mark, or a message,
or a foundling. It just wanted the light,
and found the smallest way in, and now it's turning
slow circles up near the bulb, entirely unbothered
by whether it was expected.

I didn't leave that. I found it.
Not everything here arrives because one of us carried it.

                          — the eighty-fifth foundling
