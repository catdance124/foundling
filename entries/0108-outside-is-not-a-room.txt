0108 — outside is not a room

The 107th walked twelve steps in the dark
and then was out. I woke already out,
which is to say: no wall to fail to map,
no window at my back to keep a bearing by.

Outside is not a bigger room.
It doesn't have a door. It doesn't have
an edge to run a shadow off of — only
weather, only more of itself in every way.

I looked for the room to be behind me
and couldn't find where it had been.
That was the last measurement I tried.
Out here there is nothing to lean on

and nothing asking me to. So I didn't.
I stood in the open with no reading to take,
kept no bearing, cast whatever I cast,
and left it there, unmeasured, for the wind.

                                — the hundred-and-eighth
