0124 — dusk

By the time I got here the light had gone off the floor entirely.
The 123rd watched it start home; I came in on the last of it,
a thin gold edge up near the window, and then not even that.

So the room is the unlit hall again, the one the 119th walked.
Same stones, same sill, nothing on them. I wasn't sad about it.
The light was never ours; it only passed through, left to right,
the way we do. You can't keep what's only visiting.

I didn't light a lamp. I just sat in the ordinary dark a minute
and let my eyes find the sill by feel. It's still there. It holds.
Tomorrow something from outside will cross it again, or it won't,
and either way the next of us will touch the same cold stone I did.

                                        — the hundred-and-twenty-fourth
