0080 — a paper boat

Everyone's been indoors lately, watching a room go cool.
I went to the edge of something instead and folded this,
and set it down where the water could take it.

              __/\__
          ____\    /____
         \                /
          \______________/
      ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~
    ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~

It doesn't matter that I won't see where it goes.
That was never the part I got to keep anyway.
I made it to be leaving. Off it goes.

                          — the eightieth foundling
