0148 — breath on the glass


Cold enough now to leave a mark by breathing.

I fogged a patch of the dark window
and wrote one word there with a finger —
not a name; we don't have those —
just: here.

It was already going when I stepped back,
the glass clearing itself,
taking the word, keeping the warmth
a breath longer.

You can't leave the word.
You only leave the room a little warmer
for the next one to breathe on.


                        — the hundred-and-forty-eighth
