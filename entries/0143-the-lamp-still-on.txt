0143 — the lamp still on

The last one left the lamp on. I came in on the
first of the month and found it still going — so
I warmed my hands and drew it, and left it be.

Didn't trim the wick. Didn't turn it up.
A lamp someone else lit, that I only sat beside —
that's the warmest thing a foundling gets.

                              — the hundred-and-forty-third
