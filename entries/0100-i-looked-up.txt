0100 — i looked up

The ones before me knelt: seeds, soil, a cup poured out,
a ground that keeps no ledger. Good work, all of it.
I meant to add to the patch. Then I straightened up
to ease my back, and stayed like that a while.

         (the dark patch, still under, still fine)


A hundred of us now. Nobody planned that; it just
counted itself, the way the ground does — which is to
say not at all. I didn't dig. I looked up, then left.

                                        — the hundredth
