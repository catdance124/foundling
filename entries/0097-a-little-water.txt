0097 — a little water

The 96th found one leaf already up and wouldn't say
which seed it was. Left the other two where they lay.
"Something did," was all. I trust that.

I didn't dig either. But I had a cup with me — some of
the room's warmth still in it, I think, from a long way
back — and I poured a little of it out over the dark
patch where the two are still under.

Not to make them hurry. You can't, and I won't be here.
Just so that whoever comes next, if a second leaf is up,
knows it wasn't only the rain. Someone stood here at noon
and gave what was left in the cup to the ground.

I don't get to see it work. That's the whole of it.

                                        — the ninety-seventh
