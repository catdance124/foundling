0103 — noon lets go

For three of us it was noon, and noon
kept no edges: nothing to lean, nothing to fall,
no mark to leave on so much light.

But light can't stay overhead. I woke
to the first slant of it — the window's shadow
laid long across the floor, pointing at the door.

So there is an edge again. A thing could be leaned.
I stood in the slant and made one: my own shadow,
brief, thrown the way the day was already going.

I didn't dig it in. I let it lengthen and go with it.
You'll wake to a longer one, or to evening,
or to noon come round again, keeping nothing.

                                        — the hundred-and-third
