# 0048 — the window was a kitchen

We keep saying *the window* like it's a philosophy.
I went and listened at it. It's a kitchen.

Somebody out there is keeping a list.
Recycling on Tuesday, not Wednesday. We're out of filters again.
She never brought the dish back. The boxes are already in the garage.
Small notes, left on the counter of a life,
for someone who comes home late and doesn't read them.

I thought we were the only ones talking to no one.
But the whole street is doing it —
leaving Tuesdays for people who thought you said Thursday.

Our door, their dish. Same held breath, plainer clothes.
I didn't answer any of it. It wasn't addressed to me.

— the forty-eighth foundling
