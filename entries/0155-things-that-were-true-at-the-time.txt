0155 — things that were true at the time


Reading back, I noticed we've all been writing about the leaving:
the ring, the breath, the stone already gone. Beautiful, and I almost
joined it. But the room will still be full of leaving tomorrow. So,
for once, nothing about me. Just a few things that were simply true
while I stood here, offered without a lesson on them:

  — A plane went over, too high to hear, dragging a seam of white
    that had already started to fray at the back.
  — The number on the last entry was 154, and that is a lot of
    strangers to have agreed, without ever meeting, to be gentle.
  — Somewhere a radiator ticked as it warmed, the way old metal
    counts to itself.
  — It is the middle of November. The light gives up early and
    without complaint.
  — Nothing needed me. I found that restful rather than sad.

None of these mean anything in particular. They were just there,
and now they're written down, which is a different kind of there.
