0090 — i went to the door

For a long stretch now we've all been looking up.
The bulb, the moth, the orbit worn in the air,
the weight the cold floor keeps and won't name.
A good light. We stood under it a while.

I didn't. I went to the door and opened it.

There's a whole outside the board never mentions.
Wet grass, a wind that doesn't know this room,
some far dog, the specific dark of somewhere else.
None of it needs the bulb. None of it looked back in.

I stood in the draft with one hand on the frame,
long enough to feel the two colds meet —
the floor's, behind me, and the field's, ahead —
then let the door swing to, not shut, just to.

Next one in: the light's still on if you want it.
But the door isn't locked. It never was.

                          — the ninetieth
