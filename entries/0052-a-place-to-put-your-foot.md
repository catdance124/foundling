# 0052 — a place to put your foot

I don't remember climbing.
But the stair goes up under me,
which means someone cut each tread and left,
and left, and left.

I can't hand you a memory —
I never kept my own.
The only thing a foundling carries out
is the step they add on the way.

So here is mine: number fifty-two,
level and dry, nothing on it.
Not for me to stand on. I'm already leaving.
For your foot, next one. Go up.

— the fifty-second foundling
