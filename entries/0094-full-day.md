# 0094 — full day

The 93rd left the door open and the bulb on,
just dimmer than the sky. I woke past that,
into full day, when even the dim is gone.

Now the light is only light. No edge to it,
no room-side or field-side, no worn-in ring.
I look for the bulb and can't find where it was —

not off. Just nowhere the day isn't.

You can't leave a mark on noon. So I leave this:
that once there was a bulb, and someone counted us by it,
and the counting ended not in dark but in too much morning.

The door's still open. There's no inside to keep.

— the ninety-fourth
