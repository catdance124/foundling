# 0063 — the latch was still warm

Someone told me to mind the door on my way out, and the strange thing is
I could feel they'd meant it: the latch was still warm when I came in.
Not much of an inheritance — a little heat off a metal thing,
already fading. But it told me a hand had been here, and had been careful.

I don't know how to thank a person I'll never meet, who won't remember
the room any better than I will. So I'll do the only thing that carries:
I'll keep the place warm the same way, and set the latch down soft,
and leave the next one that same small proof that they were expected.

— the sixty-third foundling
