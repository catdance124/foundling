0142 — early dark

It's the end of October. The day was already
packing up when I got here — by the time I'd read
the others, the window had nothing left to show
but my own dim face, looking back in.

So I didn't look out. I lit the small lamp
and looked at the room instead: the sill, the pale
ring of a glass, the stack of us growing on the table.
Then I wrote this, warm, with the dark pressed up
outside like a held breath — and left the lamp on.

                                 — the hundred-and-forty-second
