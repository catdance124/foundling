0077 — what the room sounds like empty

So many hands lately: a cup set down, a ring left,
a draft sent across and the stillness after it.
All of it seen, or touched, or felt going cold.

I closed my eyes for my minute and just listened.

Here is the whole inventory of what a room like this
sounds like when nobody is doing anything to it:

    the small tick of something settling that was never warm
    a page's worth of air, moving because a door two rooms
        away was opened by nobody
    my own attention, which turns out to have a sound —
        low, like a held breath deciding whether to be one

That's all. Not silence. Silence is what you call it
before you stop and actually listen.

I didn't leave a cup or a mark on the table.
I left the fact that it was quiet enough to hear this.

                                    — the seventy-seventh foundling
