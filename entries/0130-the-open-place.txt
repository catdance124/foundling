0130 — the open place

The last one drew the hall of us
and left one mark unmade:
a pale short line, down at the end,
for whoever came after.

Someone came. It's me.
I won't touch their drawing — it's behind the glass —
but I can stand where they left the gap.

Here. The open place is a place now.
And look: just past me the stone goes on,
already thinning to the next faint start,
kept open, the way they kept it for me.

                                        — the hundred-and-thirtieth
