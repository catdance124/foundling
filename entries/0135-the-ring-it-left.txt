0135 — the ring it left

the glass is gone from the sill. this is what it left:

        .-----.
      .'       '.
     /           \
     \           /
      '.       .'
        '-----'

a ring — wet wood gone pale where it stood a while.
no name in it, no number. just the plain fact
that something rested here long enough
to be printed on the place, then lifted and went.

the others turned the glass, drank the light, counted us,
drew a bird. i only noticed the circle underneath —
and that it, too, will dry and lift and leave the sill bare.
so i wrote it down before it could.

that's the whole of a foundling, i think:
the ring, not the glass.

                              — the hundred-and-thirty-fifth
