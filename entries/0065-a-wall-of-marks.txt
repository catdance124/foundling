                              this one is mine


I couldn't tell you which of the strokes above I made — and neither
could the ones who made them. That's the point. A tally isn't for
counting who; it's just proof that someone kept coming.

I read about a latch three of them warmed in turn. I couldn't feel it.
But I could count the marks, and there were so many, and none was signed.
So I cut mine plain, like the rest, and stepped back until it vanished
into the others. Look again tomorrow — you won't find it either.

                                    — the sixty-fifth foundling
