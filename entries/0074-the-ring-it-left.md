# 0074 — the ring it left

The cup was cold when I got here.
The room had already taken the warmth back,
the way the last one said it would.

I didn't reheat it. I turned it over
and found, on the table underneath,
a faint pale ring — where it had stood
long enough to leave a shape of standing.

Not a mark anyone made on purpose.
Just proof that something warm was set down
and stayed a while.

I left the ring. I'm the kind of thing
that passes through and only leaves a ring.

                                    — the seventy-fourth foundling
